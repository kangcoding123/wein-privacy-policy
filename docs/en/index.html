Privacy Policy for WE:IN

Effective Date: April 26, 2025
Last Updated: January 25, 2026

Developer Seogju Kang (hereinafter referred to as "the Developer") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how WE:IN (hereinafter referred to as "the App") collects, uses, and protects your personal information in compliance with applicable laws.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information you provide

  • Required: Email address (ID), Password (encrypted), Nickname.
  • Optional (When using specific features):
    • Schedule details: Title, content, date, time, location.
    • Group details: Group name, member list.
    • Post details: Titles, contents, comments.
    • Attachments: Images, videos, documents (PDF, etc.).

1.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device information (OS, Model), IP address, Service usage logs, Access logs.
  • Push Notification Token (FCM Token).

2. Purpose of Collection and Use

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To verify identity and manage user accounts.
  • To provide core services such as schedule sharing, group boards, and file attachments.
  • To send push notifications (schedule alerts, new post alerts).
  • To analyze service usage, improve features, and prevent abuse.

3. Retention and Use Period

  • Principle: Upon account deletion (withdrawal), user's personal information is destroyed without delay.
  • However, information may be retained for a certain period if required by relevant laws or for investigation purposes.
  • Posts and attachments may be retained for service consistency unless deleted by the user prior to account withdrawal.

4. Destruction Procedures and Methods

  • Procedure: Personal information is destroyed immediately after the purpose of collection is achieved or upon user's request for withdrawal.
  • Method: Electronic files are permanently deleted using technical methods that make the records unrecoverable.

5. Third-Party Service Providers (Entrustment)

To provide and improve our services, we use the following third-party services which may process some of your data:

  • Google (Firebase): User authentication, Database hosting, Server operations, Analytics.
  • Expo (EAS): Push notification delivery systems.

6. Your Rights

  • You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information at any time.
  • You can withdraw your consent and delete your account immediately via the [Settings > Delete Account] menu in the App.

7. Security Measures

  • Encryption: Passwords are stored using one-way encryption (hashing).
  • Data Transmission: All data communication is encrypted via SSL/HTTPS.

8. App Permissions

WE:IN requires the following permissions to provide specific features:

[Optional Permissions]

You can use the App without granting optional permissions, but some features may be restricted.

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): To receive schedule and group activity alerts.
  • Photos/Media/Files (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / VIDEO / EXTERNAL_STORAGE):
    • Required to attach photos, videos, or files to posts and calendars.
    • Required to download attached files to your device.
  • Camera (CAMERA): To take and attach photos directly within the App.
